What each stage means and common questions

We believe you should never have to guess what is happening with your job application. Here is how our 5-stage process works.

The 5 application stages

Every application moves through these five steps in order. A real person reads every application we receive.

  1. Typical duration: 1 to 2 working days

    1. Received

    Your application has arrived safely in our system and you have been given an application reference code (such as APP-1042). Our recruitment coordinator checks that all files open properly before passing your application to the hiring team. You do not need to do anything at this stage.

  2. Typical duration: 3 to 7 working days

    2. Under review

    The hiring manager for the role is carefully reading your CV, cover note, and work examples. Because we review applications thoughtfully rather than using automated rejection filters, this step takes a few days. We will always show an expected reply date on your status page.

  3. Typical duration: 5 to 10 working days

    3. Interview

    We would like to meet you! When you reach this stage, your status page will show Yes — action is needed from you along with a deadline to choose an interview time slot. We also tell you upfront who you will meet and how long the conversation will take.

  4. Typical duration: 2 to 4 working days

    4. Decision

    All interviews for the shortlist are complete and the hiring panel is meeting to make a final choice. Normally you do not need to do anything during this stage unless we ask to confirm a reference contact.

  5. Final step

    5. Outcome

    We have shared our final decision with you by email or phone—either extending a job offer or letting you know respectfully that we have chosen another candidate on this occasion. We never leave an application open without a clear answer.

Frequently asked questions

Select any question below to read the answer:

I haven't heard back yet — what should I do?

First, look up your application status. Look at the What happens next box on your status page:

If it says No under "Do I need to do anything?", your application is still being reviewed and you can relax until the expected reply date shown. If the expected reply date has passed by more than 2 working days, please send us a quick message and we will check on it immediately.

Can I update my CV, phone number, or email address after applying?

Yes. Please use our Contact Team form, include your application reference code (for example, APP-1042), and let us know what needs to be updated. Our recruitment coordinator will attach your updated details to your file within 1 working day.

I lost my application reference code — how can I find it?

Search your email inbox (and your spam or junk folder) for the subject line "Application received"—your reference starts with APP- followed by four digits. If you still cannot find it, contact the hiring team with your full name and the job title you applied for, and we will re-send your reference code.

What if I need adjustments or accessibility support for my interview?

We are happy to arrange extra time, live captions, sign-language interpretation, screen-reader friendly materials, or step-free venue access. Simply mention what helps you work best when confirming your interview slot or via our Contact Team page.

Will selecting an later interview day hurt my chances?

Not at all. Every candidate invited to interview is given fair consideration before the panel meets at the Decision stage. Pick whichever offered slot works best for your schedule before the reply deadline.

Still have a question?

Our recruitment team is here to help. Send us a message and we will reply within 2 working days.
